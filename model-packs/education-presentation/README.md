# Education Presentation Model-Pack

Shared education presentation pack for reusable narrative framing and world-sim
references.

This pack is cosmetic only. It can provide presentation assets to department
education packs, but it must not own curriculum, learning-state transitions,
standing-order selection, or escalation thresholds.

## Owns

- World-sim specs and templates under `world-sim/`
- Presentation-only consent and narrative reference material
- The `domain/edupres/presentation-core/v1` boundary physics

## Does Not Own

- Math curriculum or problem generation
- Student Commons/journaling policy
- Teacher, teaching-assistant, or domain-authority workflows
- Any learner-state or escalation logic
